AI in Healthcare

Artificial Intelligence is being used in hospitals to help doctors and improve patient care.

How is AI used?

  1. AI can help doctors analyse X-rays.
  2. AI can help identify signs of diseases.
  3. AI can help monitor patients.

Benefits to Society

AI can support faster diagnosis and help healthcare professionals make decisions.
